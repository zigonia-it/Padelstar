## Creating a Free Tournament and Joining from Another Device

### Test Scenario
Created a new cup tournament on my mother's laptop without being logged into an account. I then attempted to join the tournament from my mobile device using my own account.

### Steps
1. Created a new cup tournament on a laptop without an account.
2. Opened the tournament joining flow on a mobile device.
3. Scanned the tournament QR code.
4. Entered my name when prompted.
5. Attempted to claim the player position/user that had been created.
6. Was redirected to the landing page.

### Result
The system indicated that I had successfully joined the tournament. However, I was unable to access the tournament view afterwards.

Specifically:
- I could not access the **Player View**.
- I could not access my **matches**.
- The tournament was not accessible from my account after joining.
- Although the system displayed that I had joined successfully, the expected tournament/player experience was not available.

### Expected Result
After successfully joining the tournament, the user should be able to access the tournament view and be automatically connected to their **Player View**, including their assigned position and matches.

### Status
